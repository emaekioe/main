Game rules

Learn how to play UNO and master the action cards

How to play

🎯

Objective

Be the first player to discard all your cards.

⚙️

Configuration

Define game options such as the number of players or the number of starting cards before the game begins.

🂠

Setup

Each player receives the configured number of starting cards. The remaining cards form the draw pile, and one card is placed face up to start the discard pile.

🔄

Taking a turn

Play a card that matches the top card on the discard pile — same colour or matching calculated result. Enter the result in the input field to confirm.

➕

Drawing a card

If no valid card can be played, draw a card from the draw pile.

💻

Device handover

In multiplayer mode, the game is played locally on one device. After each turn, a handover screen appears and shows which player should receive the device next. This prevents other players from seeing the current player’s hand cards.

🔍

Card zoom

If a card is too small to read, double-click the card to enlarge it.

📣

UNO!

When you have only one card left, press the UNO button. Fail to do so and you draw two penalty cards.

🏆

Winning

The game ends as soon as one player has no cards left.

Action cards

Action cards add special effects to the game and introduce additional strategy and interaction between players. They can change the turn order, force players to draw cards, or affect the cards in a player's hand. Action cards are multi-coloured. After most action cards, the next player may play any card. The only exception is the Four-leaf clover card: after a colour has been chosen, the next player must either play a card in that colour or play another action card.

Draw two

The next player must draw two cards and miss their turn.

Draw three

The next player must draw three cards and miss their turn.

Minus one

The player must give one card from their hand to another player.

Reverse

The direction of play changes.

In a two-player game, the player who played the Reverse card gets another turn.

Four-leaf clover

The player selects the next colour to be played.

Hourglass

The next player is skipped.
